YOUR PREPARATION GUIDE

Beforeyour session.

A few small, unhurried things that help your first Quantum Healing session become all that it can be.

dodiekendall.com

Preparing for a QHHT session is mostly internal, not logistical. There's no special diet, no incense to burn, no mantra to memorize. But a few small things in the days before make a real difference in what your session can do.

1
A WEEK BEFORE

Live with your questions

QHHT is a conversation with the deepest part of you. It works because you set the agenda: the questions you bring are the doorways through which the Subconscious speaks.

This is the single most important thing.

A few days before our pre-session call, write down every question you'd ask if you knew you had a long, uninterrupted conversation with the part of yourself that holds all the answers. Don't filter. Let it be messy. Then set the list down for a day. Come back. Edit. Cross some off. Add others.

By the time we talk, you'll have five to ten questions that feel right, and together we'll narrow them to the three or four that matter most.

2
THE NIGHT BEFORE

Sleep

Real, full sleep: aim for eight hours if you can. The Theta brainwave state you'll rest in for most of the session is already deeply restful, so arriving tired risks dropping all the way to sleep instead of staying in that productive threshold.

If you're a poor sleeper, that's completely fine. We work with what you have. But if you can give yourself one good night's rest, let this be the night.

3
THE MORNING OF

Light food & water

Eat something nourishing but light: fruit, oatmeal, eggs, toast. A heavy meal slows the body and makes the Theta state harder to enter cleanly. And hydrate: drink water in the hour or two before, since you'll be lying still for a long while and won't want to start out thirsty.

GENTLY AVOID THE DAY OF

  • Caffeine: it interferes with the brain-wave shift
  • Alcohol the night before: longer half-life than you think
  • Heavy meals
  • Recreational substances
4
COMFORT

What to wear

Comfortable clothes, with layers if you tend to run cold. Nothing tight at the waist or neck, and slip-on shoes you can kick off. If you wear glasses, bring them but expect to take them off. Same for any jewelry that might press against you while you're reclining.

5
FOR AN IN-PERSON SESSION

What to bring

  • Your written question list, or just your phone with the list on it
  • A water bottle
  • A light snack for after: your blood sugar may want it
  • Anything that grounds you: a small stone, a photo, a journal. Optional.

6
THE MORNING OF, IF YOU HAVE TIME

A short grounding ritual

  1. 1Sit quietly for five minutes.
    Close your eyes. Breathe.
  2. 2Bring each top question to mind, one at a time.
    Don't try to answer them. Just hold each one for thirty seconds.
  3. 3Notice which feels heaviest, warmest, or most alive.
    That's likely the one your Higher Self has been waiting for.

You don't have to do this. But many clients say it shifts the session from "I hope something happens" to "I'm ready to listen."

7
RIGHT AFTER

Let it land

You'll feel like you woke from a long, vivid nap. Some clients are energized; some are quiet and want to drive home in silence. Both are perfectly fine. Drink water, eat something, and don't make any big decisions that day. Your audio recording will be ready shortly after, and most clients listen back over the following weeks, since insights often arrive on a delay.

One last thing

Trust your own pace. There's no right way to enter this. If you arrive nervous, we'll work with nervous. If you arrive eager, we'll work with eager. The Subconscious doesn't care about your prep score. It cares that you're here, and that you've brought it questions worth answering.
